Ledger — expense tracker

Fully clickable, four screens. Everything you tap is real: the keypad writes a transaction, the ledger and insights recompute from it, and the budget ring follows.

Tweaks
Assumptions
One person, INR, ₹40,000 monthly cap, manual keypad entry.
Warm-but-quiet copy: it encourages, it never scolds.
Nocturne's rule: the accent is a line and a glow — no saturated floods, no second hue. Categories are steps of the one accent ramp.
Variants
Home — Arc: budget ring, one glowing card, pace nudge. Ledger: big number, thin bar, three stat tiles.
Add expense — Keypad-first: amount, then a category rail. Category-first: pick the tile, then type — better for habit entry.
