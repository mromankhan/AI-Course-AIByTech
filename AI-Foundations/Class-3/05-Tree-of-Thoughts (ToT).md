# 3. Tree of Thoughts (ToT) 🌳

## What is Tree of Thoughts?

Tree of Thoughts means:

> **Instead of thinking in one way, the AI explores multiple possible solutions before choosing the best one.**

Imagine many people brainstorming together.

---

### Example

Question:

```text
How can a pizza restaurant increase sales?
```

Instead of giving one answer...

The AI creates different ideas.

---

**Expert 1**

Offer discounts.

---

**Expert 2**

Advertise on Instagram.

---

**Expert 3**

Start home delivery.

---

Then the AI compares all ideas.

Finally, it combines the best ones into one strong plan.

---

### Think of Friends

Imagine four friends solving a problem.

Each gives a different idea.

Then everyone chooses the best solution.

That's Tree of Thoughts.

---

### Easy Rule

> **Tree of Thoughts = Think of many ideas before choosing the best one.**
