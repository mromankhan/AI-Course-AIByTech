# What is Clarification-Instructions Prompting Technique?

This technique tells the AI:

> **"Don't guess. If something is unclear, ask me first."**

Instead of making assumptions, the AI asks questions before starting the task.

---

# Simple Real-Life Example

Imagine you go to a tailor and say:

> "Make me a shirt."

The tailor should not start immediately.

They should ask:

* What size?
* What color?
* Full sleeves or half sleeves?

After getting the answers, they make the correct shirt.

AI works the same way.

---

# Example Without This Technique

You say:

> "Build me a website."

The AI guesses:

* React
* Blue design
* English language

But maybe you wanted:

* Shopify
* Black design
* Arabic language

The AI guessed wrong.

---

# Example With This Technique

You say:

> "Build me a website."

The AI replies:

> Before I start, I have a few questions:
>
> * What type of website do you need?
> * Do you want Shopify or a custom website?
> * What colors do you prefer?

After you answer, the AI builds exactly what you want.

---

# Why is this Technique Useful?

It helps the AI:

* ✅ Avoid guessing
* ✅ Understand your requirements
* ✅ Ask only important questions
* ✅ Give better and more accurate results

---

# How It Works

1. Read the prompt carefully.
2. Check if any information is missing.
3. If something is unclear, ask questions.
4. Wait for the user's answers.
5. Complete the task.

---

# Simple Example

### ❌ Without the Technique

**User:**

> Write an email.

The AI has to guess:

* Who is the email for?
* What is it about?
* Formal or informal?

The result may be wrong.

---

### ✅ With the Technique

**User:**

> Write an email.

**AI:**

> Before I write the email, I need a few details:
>
> * Who is the email for?
> * What is the purpose?
> * Should it be formal or friendly?

Now the AI has enough information to write a much better email.

---

# When Should You Use It?

Use it for tasks like:

* Writing emails
* Writing proposals
* Building websites
* Generating code
* Creating business plans
* Designing AI systems
* Any complex task

---

# When Don't You Need It?

For very simple tasks like:

* "Translate this sentence."
* "What is 2 + 2?"
* "Summarize this paragraph."

There is usually no need to ask questions.

---

# One-Line Summary

**This prompting technique tells the AI to ask questions first whenever information is missing or unclear, instead of guessing. This helps produce more accurate and reliable results.**
