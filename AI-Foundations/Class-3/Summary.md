
# Final Summary

| Technique                  | Simple Meaning                                                          | Example                                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Zero-Shot Prompting**    | Ask the AI directly without giving examples.                            | "Translate this sentence into French."                                    |
| **Few-Shot Prompting**     | Show a few examples before asking the real question.                    | Give 2–3 sentiment examples, then ask the AI to classify a new review.    |
| **Chain of Thought (CoT)** | Ask the AI to think step by step.                                       | Solve a math problem by showing each step.                                |
| **ReAct (Reason + Act)**   | The AI thinks, uses tools if needed, observes the result, then answers. | Search live weather before replying.                                      |
| **Tree of Thoughts (ToT)** | Explore multiple ideas or solutions before choosing the best one.       | Brainstorm different business strategies and combine the strongest ideas. |
| **Directional Stimulus**   | Give hints about what the AI should focus on.                           | "Summarize this report. Focus on revenue and expenses."                   |
| **Iterative Prompting**    | Improve your prompt again and again until you get the desired output.   | Add more details, constraints, and formatting after each attempt.         |

## One sentence to remember

> **Zero-Shot asks directly, Few-Shot teaches with examples, Chain of Thought solves step by step, ReAct thinks and uses tools, Tree of Thoughts explores multiple solutions, Directional Stimulus guides the AI's focus, and Iterative Prompting keeps improving the prompt until the answer is exactly what you need.**
