# 1. Chain of Thought (CoT) 🪜

## What is Chain of Thought?

Chain of Thought means:

> **Ask the AI to think step by step before answering.**

Instead of giving a quick answer...

The AI explains each step.

---

### Example

Question:

```text
Ali has 5 apples.

He buys 3 more.

Then gives 2 to his friend.

How many apples are left?

Think step by step.
```

AI thinks:

Step 1:

5 apples

↓

Step 2:

5 + 3 = 8

↓

Step 3:

8 - 2 = 6

↓

Final Answer:

```text
6 apples
```

---

### Why Use It?

It helps with:

* Math
* Accounting
* Finance
* Logic
* Programming

Because the AI is less likely to make mistakes.

---

### Easy Rule

> **Chain of Thought = Solve the problem one step at a time.**
