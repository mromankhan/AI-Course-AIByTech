# Learning Techniques

Learning techniques are different ways of asking the AI to perform a task.

Think of it like teaching a student.

Sometimes you ask directly.

Sometimes you show examples first.

---

# 1. Zero-Shot Prompting 🎯

## What is Zero-Shot Prompting?

Zero-Shot Prompting means:

> **You ask the AI to do something without giving any examples.**

The AI uses its own knowledge to answer.

---

### Example

You ask:

```text
Translate this into French:

Hello, how are you?
```

You didn't show any example.

The AI already knows how to translate.

---

Another Example

```text
Classify this review as Positive or Negative:

"The food was delicious."
```

The AI understands and answers:

```text
Positive
```

No examples were given.

---

### Think of a Teacher

A teacher asks:

> "What is 5 + 5?"

The student already knows the answer.

The teacher doesn't need to show examples.

That's Zero-Shot Prompting.

---

### Easy Rule

> **Zero-Shot = No examples, just ask.**
