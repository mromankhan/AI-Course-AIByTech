# 1. Directional Stimulus (Hint-Based Prompting) 💡

## What is Directional Stimulus?

Directional Stimulus means:

> **Give the AI hints about what to focus on.**

Instead of saying:

```text
Summarize this report.
```

You say:

```text
Summarize this report.

Focus on:

- Sales
- Profit
- Expenses
```

Now the AI knows what is important.

---

### Another Example

Instead of asking:

```text
Explain this company.
```

Ask:

```text
Explain this company.

Focus only on:

- Revenue
- Customers
- Future plans
```

The answer becomes much more focused.

---

### Think of a Highlighter

Imagine reading a book.

Someone highlights only the important lines.

You pay attention to those lines.

Directional Stimulus works the same way.

---

### Easy Rule

> **Directional Stimulus = Give the AI hints about what to focus on.**
