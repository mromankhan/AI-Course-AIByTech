# 2. Few-Shot Prompting 📚

## What is Few-Shot Prompting?

Few-Shot Prompting means:

> **You give the AI a few examples before asking the real question.**

This helps the AI understand:

* your format
* your style
* your expectations

---

### Example

Instead of saying:

```text
Classify this review.
```

You write:

```text
Input:
I love this phone.

Output:
Positive

-------------------

Input:
This laptop is terrible.

Output:
Negative

-------------------

Input:
The movie was amazing.

Output:
?
```

Now the AI understands the pattern.

It replies:

```text
Positive
```

---

### Think of a Child

Imagine teaching a child.

You say:

Cat → Animal

Dog → Animal

Rose → Plant

Now ask:

Apple → ?

The child understands the pattern.

The AI works similarly.

---

### Easy Rule

> **Few-Shot = Show a few examples first, then ask the real question.**
